Seafood Paella with Saffron

by Chef Thimira Nimsara verified

March 20, 2025
schedule 45 mins group 4 servings local_fire_department 560 cal

A classic Spanish dish packed with seafood, saffron-infused rice, and aromatic spices. Perfect for special occasions!

Main Course Seafood Gluten Free

Ingredients

2 cups Bomba rice
4 cups seafood stock
500g mixed seafood
3 tbsp olive oil
1 onion, finely chopped
Salt and black pepper to taste
3 cloves garlic, minced
Fresh parsley, chopped
1 red bell pepper, sliced
Lemon wedges, for serving
1/2 tsp saffron threads

Instructions

1
Sauté aromatics

Heat olive oil in a large paella pan. Add onion, garlic, and bell pepper. Sauté until soft.

2
Cook seafood

Add seafood and cook until just turned pink. Remove and set aside.

3
Toast rice

Stir in rice and saffron threads. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring well.

4
Add stock

Pour in warm seafood stock. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a simmer.

5
Simmer

Cook uncovered for 18-20 minutes until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.

6
Rest & serve

Let rest for 5 minutes. Top with seafood and parsley. Serve with lemon wedges.
